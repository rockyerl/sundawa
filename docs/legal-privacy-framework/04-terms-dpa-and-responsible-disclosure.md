# 04 — Terms of Use, Disclaimers, B2B DPA & Responsible Disclosure

## 1. Terms of Use (`/terms-of-use`)
- Governs access to `sundawa.net`, interactive estimators, diagnostic tools, and executive guides.
- Clarifies that online estimates (Outsource Estimator, Legacy System Cost Calculator, System Health Assessment) are **non-binding informational models** and do not constitute formal commercial proposals.
- Protects PT Sundawa Teknologi Indonesia's intellectual property while noting client custom work ownership is governed by executed Master Services Agreements (MSA).
- Governing law: Laws of the Republic of Indonesia.

## 2. Legal Disclaimer (`/disclaimer`)
- Clarifies that financial models, benchmark scenarios, and checklists are intended for preliminary B2B evaluation.
- Reaffirms non-certification transparency for ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 25010, OWASP, and COBIT 2019/7 internal reference practices.

## 3. B2B Data Processing Agreement (`/dpa`)
- Explains Sundawa's standard B2B DPA structure when PT Sundawa Teknologi Indonesia acts as a **Data Processor** on behalf of an enterprise client (**Data Controller**).
- Covers processing instructions, confidentiality, Technical & Organizational Measures (TOMs), sub-processor approval, breach notification support, and return/deletion of client data upon contract end.
- Request via `contact@sundawa.net` with subject `[Legal / DPA]`.

## 4. Responsible Disclosure Policy (`/responsible-disclosure`)
- Invites good-faith security researchers to report vulnerabilities affecting `sundawa.net` or public Sundawa portals to `contact@sundawa.net` with subject `[Security Disclosure]`.
- Safe harbor for authorized good-faith research; explicitly prohibits Denial-of-Service (DoS), social engineering, automated destructive scanning, or accessing/exfiltrating other users' data.
- Target initial acknowledgment: **3 business days**.
