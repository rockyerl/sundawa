## Regulatory & Governance Reference Matrix

| Framework / Regulation | Scope & Operational Relevance |
|---|---|
| **UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 (UU PDP)** | Primary statutory personal data protection framework in the Republic of Indonesia (transparency, lawful basis, data subject rights, retention limits). |
| **EU GDPR & UK GDPR** | Transparency, opt-in consent, international transfer disclosures, and data subject rights when serving UK/European prospects and partners. |
| **ePrivacy Directive & UK PECR** | Consent-gated non-essential analytics/marketing cookies with equal-prominence **Reject All** / **Customize** / **Accept All** controls. |
| **ISO/IEC 27001 & ISO/IEC 27701** | Internal engineering reference controls for information security and privacy governance (non-certification alignment). |
| **Google Ads & GA4 Policies** | Mandatory bilingual Privacy Policy link on lead-capture pages and consent-gated analytics execution. |
