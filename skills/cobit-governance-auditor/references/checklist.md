# COBIT Governance Auditor — field checklist

| Step | Verification | Minimum evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise goals | Business-to-IT value and risk priorities linked | approved goal register | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Scoping | Selected governance objectives and exclusions justified | scope and charter | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Accountabilities | Governing vs management functions documented; one A per key activity | signed RACI | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Risk methodology | Scoring, thresholds, owner and appetite approved | dated risk register, approvals | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Risk treatment | High risks addressed with verified mitigation or unexpired acceptance | change/test record or exception | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Process readiness | Every selected objective has process-level checks and review | capability assessment + evidence | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Monitoring | Control check scheduled and performed with independent evidence | monitoring records | pass/fail/not-tested |
| Assurance | Reviewer, finding IDs, corrective actions and follow-up logged | audit report and tickets | pass/fail/not-tested |

Use ISACA COBIT 7 official references for detailed activities and formal assessments. The scores and gates here are Sundawa internal policy and **not** official ISACA ratings.
